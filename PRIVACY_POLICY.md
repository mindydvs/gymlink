# GymLink Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 19, 2026

GymLink ("we," "us," or "our") operates the GymLink mobile and web applications (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using GymLink, you agree to the practices described below.

If you have questions, contact us at **mindydvs@yahoo.com**.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you create and use a GymLink account:

**Account information you provide**
- Name
- Age
- Password (stored in encrypted form using bcrypt — we never see or store your plaintext password)
- Profile bio
- Gym you attend
- Workout interests
- Profile photo
- Workout videos you upload (limited to 2 minutes each)

**Activity information**
- Check-ins at your gym
- Connection requests you send and receive (crush, buddy, advisor, spotter)
- Notifications generated for you
- Likes on workout videos

**Technical information**
- Device type and operating system
- Basic app usage data needed to keep the Service running
- Crash and error logs

We do **not** collect precise location data, contacts, browsing history outside the app, or financial information.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Create and maintain your account
- Show you other GymLink members at your gym
- Let you send and receive connection requests
- Display profile photos and workout videos to other members
- Send you notifications about activity that involves you
- Keep the Service secure and prevent abuse
- Improve the app and fix bugs

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use your data for advertising.

---

## 3. Information Shared with Other Users

The following information is visible to other GymLink members:
- Your name, age, bio, gym, interests, profile photo, and workout videos
- Whether you are currently checked in at the gym

Connection requests are visible only to you and the user you send them to.

Your password, email, and any other account-recovery information are never shown to other users.

---

## 4. How We Store and Protect Your Information

- Account data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL database
- Profile photos and workout videos are stored in encrypted object storage
- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before being stored
- We use HTTPS for all communication between the app and our servers

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no system is 100% secure. You use the Service at your own risk.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

You can at any time:
- **View and edit** your profile information in the app
- **Delete content** you have uploaded (profile photos, workout videos)
- **Delete your account** by emailing us at mindydvs@yahoo.com — we will remove your account and associated personal data within 30 days

If you live in the EU, UK, or California, you also have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, or to request its deletion. To exercise these rights, email us at mindydvs@yahoo.com.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

GymLink is intended for users **17 years of age or older**. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 17. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

---

- **Replit** — application hosting and database (https://replit.com/site/privacy)

---

## 8. Data Retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it (for example, basic transaction records).

---

## 9. International Users

GymLink is operated from the United States. If you use the Service from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and stored in the U.S.
